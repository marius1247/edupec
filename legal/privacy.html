EDUPEC Ltd (“EDUPEC”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the EDUPEC mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and your rights regarding that data. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Data Controller

EDUPEC Ltd is the data controller for personal data processed through the App. We are registered in England and Wales. For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at contact@edupec.uk.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account & Authentication Data

When you create an account, we collect: email address, password (never stored in plain text — hashed by Supabase Auth), OAuth tokens (when signing in via Google or Apple), and a randomly generated device identifier used as an anonymous session identifier before account creation.

2.2 Onboarding & Profile Data

During onboarding, you provide: display name, age category, current English level (A1–C2 CEFR), learning goal, weekly study goal, and native language.

2.3 Learning Progress Data

As you use the App, the following is recorded: vocabulary knowledge scores and spaced repetition schedules, saved/hidden words, grammar lesson completions and bookmarks, daily study streak, streak freeze balance, coin balance, free books balance, double rewards status, and weekly goal progress.

2.4 Writing Session Data

When you use the Writing Practice feature, submitted text is sent to our AI provider for automated feedback. If you save a session, topic title, your text, word count, AI feedback, and timestamp are stored locally on your device — not on our servers. Your submitted text is not used to train AI models.

2.5 Reading & Offline Data (Stored Locally)

The following data is stored locally on your device only: reading positions per book, saved books, grammar lesson cache, vocabulary content cache, session drafts, and the offline sync queue.

2.6 Subscription & Purchase Data

Premium subscription purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) and managed via RevenueCat. We receive only confirmation of your active entitlement — we do not receive your payment card details or billing address.

2.7 Technical & Diagnostic Data

  • App version and operating system version
  • Device type and platform (iOS or Android)
  • Crash reports and error logs
  • Network connectivity status

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you securely
  • To personalise your learning experience, including spaced repetition scheduling and CEFR level recommendations
  • To track and synchronise your learning progress, study streak, and Coin balance
  • To enable offline learning and sync your progress when you reconnect
  • To manage your premium subscription status
  • To provide AI writing feedback by transmitting your submitted text to our AI subprocessor
  • To provide text-to-speech audio pronunciations using the ElevenLabs service
  • To send important service notifications and account-related communications
  • To analyse aggregate, anonymised usage patterns and improve the App
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Where the UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies, we process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)) — processing necessary to provide the App’s core services.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — improving the App, preventing fraud, ensuring service security, and analysing aggregate usage patterns.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) — where retention or disclosure of data is required by applicable law.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — for optional features such as marketing communications or push notifications. You may withdraw consent at any time.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

  • Supabase, Inc. — backend database and authentication. Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. — subscription management. Privacy Policy.
  • ElevenLabs, Inc. — text-to-speech audio. Privacy Policy.
  • AI writing feedback subprocessor — submitted writing text is processed solely to produce feedback and is not linked to your account identity or used for AI training.
  • Apple Inc. — App Store distribution, Apple Sign In, and in-app purchases. Privacy Policy.
  • Google LLC — Google Play distribution, Google Sign In, and in-app purchases. Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party. We do not use your personal data for advertising or tracking purposes.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, we will delete or anonymise your data within 30 days, except where retention is required by applicable law (financial/tax records may be retained for up to 7 years under UK law). Locally cached data is cleared after 24 hours or when you uninstall the App.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 where UK/EU GDPR applies without verified parental consent). If you believe your child has provided personal data without your consent, contact us at contact@edupec.uk.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the following rights: access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), objection (Art. 21), rights regarding automated decision-making (Art. 22), and the right to lodge a complaint with the ICO at ico.org.uk. Contact us at contact@edupec.uk to exercise any right.

9. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), hashed password storage via Supabase Auth, row-level security (RLS) on our database, and session token management. In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside the UK or EEA, including the United States. Such transfers are carried out using appropriate safeguards — including UK IDTAs or EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs). Contact us at contact@edupec.uk to request a copy of the applicable transfer mechanism.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via in-app notification or email at least 14 days before changes take effect.
