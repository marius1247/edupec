EDUPEC Ltd ("EDUPEC", "we", "us", or "our") operates the EDUPEC mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and your rights regarding that data. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Data Controller

EDUPEC Ltd is the data controller for personal data processed through the App. We are registered in England and Wales. For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at privacy@edupec.com.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account & Authentication Data

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address — provided directly during email/password registration, or retrieved from your Google or Apple account when you use OAuth sign-in.
  • Password — never stored in plain text. Passwords are handled entirely by Supabase Auth using industry-standard cryptographic hashing. We cannot read your password.
  • OAuth tokens — when you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive a short-lived authentication token from that provider to verify your identity. We do not receive your Google or Apple password.
  • Device identifier (Device ID) — a randomly generated identifier stored on your device, used as an anonymous session identifier before you create or sign into an account. This allows the App to track learning progress locally without requiring registration.

2.2 Onboarding & Profile Data

During onboarding, you provide the following information, which is stored in your user profile on our servers:

  • Display name
  • Age category
  • Current English level (A1–C2 CEFR)
  • Learning goal
  • Weekly study goal (number of sessions per week)
  • Native language / mother tongue (used to display vocabulary translations in your language)

2.3 Learning Progress Data

As you use the App, the following data is recorded and stored on our servers to personalise your experience and maintain continuity across devices:

  • Vocabulary knowledge scores per word and spaced repetition review schedules (knowledgeScore, nextReviewAt)
  • Words saved to your personal collection and words you have chosen to hide ("disliked")
  • Word review counts and session history
  • Grammar lessons completed, lessons marked for review ("Add to Review" bookmarks), and progress per lesson
  • Daily study streak, longest streak, and streak history
  • Streak Freeze balance (consumable items that protect your streak on missed days)
  • Coin balance (in-app virtual currency)
  • XP (experience points) balance
  • Free Books balance (credits to unlock reading content)
  • Double Rewards status (temporary 2× coin multiplier)
  • Weekly study goal progress (sessions and individual words studied per week)

2.4 Writing Session Data

When you use the Writing Practice feature, you may submit a text response to an AI-powered prompt. The submitted text is sent to our AI provider for automated feedback generation. If you choose to save a writing session, the following data is stored locally on your device (not on our servers unless explicitly stated):

  • Writing topic title
  • Your written response (full text)
  • Word count
  • AI feedback results (overall grade, CEFR level estimate, grammar errors, vocabulary/coherence/grammar/task achievement scores, suggested next level, detailed written feedback)
  • Session timestamp

Writing history is saved locally on your device using encrypted local storage. It is not uploaded to our servers.

2.5 Reading & Offline Data (Stored Locally)

The following data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers:

  • Reading position — your current page number per book, stored in device AsyncStorage as reader_page_{bookId}.
  • Saved books — books you have bookmarked in the Library.
  • Grammar lesson cache — recently accessed grammar lessons and exercises are cached for offline use. The content cached depends on your subscription tier (free or premium).
  • Vocabulary content cache — vocabulary data cached for offline sessions, cleared after 24 hours.
  • Session draft — if a vocabulary training session is interrupted, an in-progress session draft is saved locally and automatically restored when you return.
  • Offline sync queue — learning actions performed while offline (e.g., completing a grammar lesson) are stored locally and automatically synced to our servers when you reconnect to the internet.

2.6 Subscription & Purchase Data

Premium subscription purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) and managed via RevenueCat. We receive confirmation of your active entitlement (i.e., whether you have an active premium subscription) but do not receive your payment card details, billing address, or full transaction records. All payment data is processed and held by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat in accordance with their own privacy policies.

2.7 Technical & Diagnostic Data

  • App version and operating system version
  • Device type and platform (iOS or Android)
  • Crash reports and error logs
  • Network connectivity status (online/offline)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you securely via email/password, Google, or Apple sign-in
  • To personalise your learning experience, including your spaced repetition schedule, vocabulary difficulty, and CEFR level recommendations
  • To track and synchronise your learning progress, study streak, XP, and coin balance across sessions and devices
  • To enable offline learning by caching content locally on your device and syncing your progress when you reconnect
  • To manage your premium subscription status and grant associated benefits (unlimited vocabulary sessions, monthly Streak Freeze allocations, enhanced offline content access)
  • To provide AI writing feedback by transmitting your submitted text to our AI provider for automated analysis (see Section 5)
  • To provide text-to-speech audio pronunciations using the ElevenLabs service (see Section 5)
  • To gate access to premium and free-tier reading content based on your subscription status and Free Books balance
  • To send important service notifications and account-related communications
  • To analyse aggregate, anonymised usage patterns and improve the App
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

4. App Features

  • Grammar Lessons — 2,200+ structured lessons organised by grammatical topic (verb forms, conditionals, modals, articles, relative clauses, advanced rhetoric, and style). Free users have access to a rotating "Free this month" selection. Full library access requires a Premium subscription or Free Books balance credits. Reading position is saved locally per book.

Submissions receive automated feedback including an overall grade (A–D), CEFR level estimate, grammar error annotations, and scores for vocabulary, coherence, grammar, and task achievement. Writing sessions may be saved locally to your device.

6. Streak System & Streak Freezes

The App tracks your daily study streak — the number of consecutive calendar days on which you have completed at least one qualifying learning session. A qualifying session includes completing a vocabulary training session, a grammar lesson, a writing exercise, or another activity designated as streak-eligible in the App.

If you do not complete a qualifying session on a given calendar day, your streak is at risk of being reset to zero. Streak Freezes allow you to protect your streak against missed days:

  • Each Streak Freeze protects against one missed day.
  • Free users may earn Streak Freezes through learning activity or by watching rewarded content.
  • Premium subscribers receive a monthly allocation of Streak Freezes automatically upon their first login each month.
  • When you sign in after missing one or more days, the App will prompt you to use available Streak Freezes — one per missed day. If you lack sufficient Streak Freezes or choose not to use them, your streak will reset to zero.

Streak resets are permanent. EDUPEC accepts no responsibility for streak losses resulting from missed sessions, device failure, loss of internet connectivity, App outages, system clock changes, timezone changes, or any other cause. Streak data is the result of your own learning behaviour and EDUPEC makes no representations about its long-term availability.
