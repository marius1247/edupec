Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · EDUPEC Ltd

EDUPEC Ltd ("EDUPEC", "we", "us", or "our") operates the EDUPEC mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and your rights regarding that data.

1. Data Controller

EDUPEC Ltd is the data controller for personal data processed through the App. We are registered in England and Wales. You can contact us at privacy@edupec.com with any privacy-related questions.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Data

  • Email address — provided when you register with email and password, or retrieved from your Google or Apple account if you use OAuth sign-in.
  • Password — never stored in plain text; handled entirely by Supabase Auth using industry-standard hashing.
  • OAuth tokens — when you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive an authentication token from that provider. We do not receive your Google or Apple password.

2.2 Onboarding & Profile Data

During onboarding, you provide the following information which is stored in your user profile on our servers:

  • Display name
  • Age category
  • Current English level (A1–C2)
  • Learning goal
  • Weekly study goal (number of sessions per week)
  • Native language / mother tongue (used to display translations)

2.3 Learning Progress Data

As you use the App, we record and store the following on our servers:

  • Vocabulary knowledge scores per word and review history
  • Daily study streak, longest streak, and streak history
  • Streak freeze balance (consumable items that protect your streak)
  • Coin balance (in-app currency earned through learning activity)
  • Free Books balance (credits to unlock reading content)
  • Double Rewards status (temporary 2x coin multiplier)
  • Words saved to your collection and words you have chosen to hide ("disliked")
  • Grammar lessons recently accessed
  • Writing session content and AI feedback (where you choose to save sessions)

2.4 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores data locally on your device to support offline use:

  • SQLite database — cached lesson and vocabulary content (tagged by your subscription tier: free or premium), pending sync actions (operations performed while offline that are sent to our servers when you reconnect), and recently accessed grammar lessons.
  • AsyncStorage cache — React Query results (vocabulary data, lesson content) are cached for up to 24 hours to reduce network requests and allow offline access.

This data is stored on your device and is not accessible to EDUPEC unless explicitly synced to our servers.

2.5 Subscription & Purchase Data

Subscription and purchase transactions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) and managed via RevenueCat. We receive confirmation of your active entitlements (e.g., whether you have an active premium subscription) but do not receive your payment card details. RevenueCat processes data in accordance with its own Privacy Policy.

2.6 Technical & Diagnostic Data

  • App version and operating system version
  • Device type and platform (iOS or Android)
  • Crash reports and error logs
  • Network connectivity status

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you securely
  • To personalise your learning experience, including your spaced repetition schedule and difficulty level
  • To track your progress, study streak, XP, and coin balance across sessions and devices
  • To enable offline learning by caching content locally and syncing your progress when you reconnect
  • To manage your premium subscription status and grant associated benefits (e.g., monthly streak freeze grants, unlimited vocabulary sessions, offline content downloads)
  • To provide AI writing feedback (your submitted text is sent to our AI provider for processing — see Section 5)
  • To send important service and account notifications
  • To analyse aggregate usage patterns and improve the App
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Where GDPR applies, we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance — processing necessary to provide the App's core services to you
  • Legitimate interests — improving the App, fraud prevention, and service security
  • Legal obligation — where required by applicable law
  • Consent — where you have explicitly consented (e.g., optional marketing notifications)

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We share data with the following third parties who process it on our behalf:

  • Supabase — our backend database and authentication provider. Your account data, profile, and learning progress are stored on Supabase infrastructure. Supabase is hosted on AWS and processes data in accordance with GDPR.
  • RevenueCat — subscription and in-app purchase management. RevenueCat receives your app user ID and communicates with Apple and Google to verify your subscription status.
  • Apple Inc. — App Store distribution, Apple Sign In authentication (iOS), and in-app purchase processing.
  • Google LLC — Google Play distribution, Google Sign In authentication, and in-app purchase processing (Android).
  • AI writing feedback provider — text you submit in the writing practice feature is sent to an AI provider for feedback generation. This text is not linked to your identity beyond the current session unless you save the session.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party. We do not use your data for advertising purposes.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by applicable law (e.g., financial records). Locally cached data on your device is automatically cleared after 24 hours (React Query cache) or when you uninstall the App.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or under 16 where GDPR applies). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below these ages. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, please contact us at privacy@edupec.com and we will promptly delete that data.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — the right to request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Rectification — the right to correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — the right to request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restriction — the right to restrict how we process your data
  • Portability — the right to receive your data in a portable format
  • Objection — the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, the right to withdraw it at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@edupec.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organisational security measures, including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), hashed password storage, and access controls on our backend infrastructure. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your own, including the United States (where Supabase/AWS and RevenueCat infrastructure may be located). Such transfers are carried out in accordance with applicable data protection law, using standard contractual clauses or other appropriate safeguards where required.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via in-app notification or email. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

When you sign in after missing days, the App will prompt you to use your available Streak Freezes. One Streak Freeze is consumed per missed day.
