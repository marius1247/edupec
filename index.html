Our Mission

Education should be
engaging, not exhausting

EDUPEC Ltd is a UK-registered educational technology company. We design and build mobile-first learning apps that adapt to each learner — delivering the right content at the right moment, powered by science-backed algorithms and artificial intelligence.

Our flagship product, FinallyFluent, is an English learning app built for Romanian, Ukrainian, and Eastern European learners who are serious about fluency. It features 3,000+ phrasal verbs, A1–C2 grammar lessons, AI writing feedback, and a gamified streak system — all in one app, launching soon on iOS and Android.
